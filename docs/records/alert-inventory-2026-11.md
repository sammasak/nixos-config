# Alert inventory, November 2026

Part of [#624](https://github.com/sammasak/nixos-config/issues/624) (item 624-a), under [#600](https://github.com/sammasak/nixos-config/issues/600). Read from source at the time of writing; nothing was queried live, so every "last receipt" is "not checked". A missing rule is a finding, not a verdict that the failure is undetected elsewhere.

## Route, common to every row

Prometheus (black-pi, `modules/homelab/monitoring/prometheus.nix`) evaluates `modules/homelab/monitoring/rules/*.yaml` and the Nix-rendered `shipping.nix`/`homelab.nix`. Alertmanager (`modules/homelab/monitoring/alertmanager.nix`) groups by namespace, alertname and severity (group_wait 30s, repeat 12h) and posts a webhook with `send_resolved = true` to alert-bridge on lenovo (`modules/homelab/alert-bridge.nix`, :9099). The bridge publishes to the ntfy topic `homelab-alerts` on lenovo's ntfy (`modules/homelab/ntfy.nix`).

Silenced before the bridge (null receiver): `Watchdog`, `InfoInhibitor`, `HostExpectedOff`, any rule with `notify="none"`, and any `severity="info"` (so `NodeCPUHighUsage` never pages). `team="shipping"` alerts are muted during quiet hours and sent when they end. Inhibition: critical over warning/info and warning over info per alertname and namespace; `HostExpectedOff` inhibits alerts carrying the same `node` and `TargetDown` for `node-exporter`/`nvidia-gpu`.

## Findings for follow-up PRs

- No rule exists for: OTLP rejection (relay: no metric at all), the CPU-accounting ratio, Authentik task-log errors, PSI, backup freshness, journal delivery. Backup and journal metrics exist and are collected, only the rules are missing.
- Metric names to build on: backup `homelab_backup_*` and journal `homelab_journal_*` as listed above, both via node-exporter job `node-exporter` with a `node` label; Alloy `otelcol_receiver_refused_*`/`otelcol_receiver_failed_*` on job `alloy-otlp`.
- Not checked: any live value or timestamp, whether the PSI collector is on, the exact Authentik log line, the installed generation versus this source, and whether any alert has ever reached ntfy.
